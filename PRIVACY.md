# Cadence Privacy Policy

_Effective October 6, 2026_

Cadence is a music player for Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic-compatible servers
that you run or have access to. It is built so that your music and your data stay
between you and your own server.

## The short version

- Cadence has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- The developer of Cadence does not collect, receive or store any of your data.
- The app talks to your music server, plus a couple of optional services described
  below, and nothing else.

## Data stored on your device

- **Server address, username and password.** Used only to sign in to your server.
  On iPhone they are kept in the iOS Keychain. They are never sent anywhere except
  to the server you entered.
- **Settings, play queue, favorites, cached artwork and downloaded songs.** Kept
  on your device so the app works quickly and offline. Deleting the app removes
  them.

## Data sent to your music server

Cadence connects to the server address you enter, to browse your library, stream
and download music, load artwork and lyrics, and manage playlists and favorites.
With **Scrobble Plays** turned on (the default), it also tells your server which
songs you play. Your server may forward this to Last.fm or ListenBrainz if you have
set that up there. How your server handles this data is governed by whoever
operates it, not by Cadence.

## Optional third-party services

- **LRCLIB (lrclib.net)**: when **Find Lyrics on LRCLIB** is turned on (the
  default) and your server has no lyrics for a song, Cadence sends that song's
  title, artist, album and length to LRCLIB to look up lyrics. No information
  about you or your account is included. You can turn this off in Settings.
- **Apple Music animated artwork**: when **Animated Artwork** is turned on (it's
  off by default), Cadence sends album titles and artist names to Apple's music
  catalog (music.apple.com) to find animated covers, and downloads those videos
  from Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to those requests:
  https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

These requests go directly from your device to the service. Neither one goes
through, or is visible to, the developer of Cadence.

## Children

Cadence doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.
